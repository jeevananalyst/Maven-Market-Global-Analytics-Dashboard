## 📊 Project Overview

**Maven Market Global Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive **Power BI business intelligence solution** developed to analyze retail performance across multiple dimensions.

The dashboard transforms raw retail data into an interactive analytical platform that enables users to monitor **sales, revenue, profit, customers, products, stores, and geographic performance**.

The project focuses on converting business data into meaningful insights through **data preparation, data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, and interactive visualizations**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to provide a centralized dashboard that helps business stakeholders:

* Monitor overall retail performance
* Track key business KPIs
* Analyze sales and revenue trends
* Evaluate product and brand performance
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Compare store and regional performance
* Identify high-performing and underperforming areas
* Explore business trends through interactive filters
* Support data-driven decision-making

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project is based on the **Maven Market retail dataset**, containing information related to the organization's retail operations.

The data supports analysis across areas such as:

* 🛒 Products
* 👥 Customers
* 🏪 Stores
* 🌎 Regions / Geography
* 📅 Dates
* 💰 Sales
* 📦 Product Categories / Brands

The dataset was transformed and modeled in Power BI to create an analysis-ready business intelligence solution.

---

## 🔄 Data Preparation

**Power Query** was used to prepare the raw data before analysis.

Key data preparation activities included:

* Removing duplicate records
* Handling missing values
* Standardizing data types
* Cleaning inconsistent values
* Renaming and organizing fields
* Creating required transformations
* Preparing tables for data modeling
* Validating data for analytical use

---

## 🧩 Data Model

A structured relational data model was developed to connect the major business entities.

The model supports analysis across:

```text
Customers
    │
    ├── Sales ─── Products
    │
    └── Stores ─── Geography

Dates
    │
    └── Sales
```

The model was designed to enable consistent filtering and aggregation across customers, products, stores, geography, and time.
